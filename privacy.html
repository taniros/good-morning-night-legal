Privacy Policy

Good Morning Night (iOS) · Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what data the app and its partners may collect, and how you can control it.

What the app does

Good Morning Night shows morning and night wallpapers, lets you favorite and save images, craft quotes on photos, and send love cards. Optional In‑App Purchases can remove ads or unlock packs.

Photos

If you create from a photo or save a wallpaper, we use the system Photos permission. Photos stay on your device unless you choose to share them. We do not upload your photo library to our servers for advertising.

Advertising

The free experience may show ads via Google AdMob. The advertising SDK may collect device identifiers (such as IDFA if you allow tracking), advertising data, coarse location derived from IP, crash/diagnostic information, and product interaction data used for third‑party advertising and analytics, as described by Google’s App Store data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

On iOS we may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. You can refuse tracking and still use the app; ads may be less relevant.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive purchase status to unlock features. We do not receive your full payment card number.

Network content

Wallpaper catalogs and Fresh scenes are loaded from our content servers so the library can update. Requests include standard technical data (IP address, user agent) needed to deliver files.

Your choices

  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • iOS Settings → Good Morning Night → Photos
  • Buy Remove ads for a quieter experience

Children

The app is rated for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Do not provide personal information about children.
